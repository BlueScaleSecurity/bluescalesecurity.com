About

BlueScale Security is Justin Board’s defensive cybersecurity practice in West Texas — built for oil & gas, agriculture, and local SMBs.

Who we are

Justin Board runs BlueScale Security. He was raised in the Permian Basin and has been around oilfield and West Texas life his whole life. He brings over ten years of systems engineering experience — hands-on managing compliance, disaster recovery, networking, identity and access (IAM), and business continuity plans (BCPs). The work stays practical: help shops tighten what matters without theater or fearmongering.

Who it’s for

Operators and owners in oil & gas, agriculture, and local SMBs who need defensive cybersecurity next to an MSP — not a full help-desk MSP replacement.

What we focus on

  • Security assessment & hardening
  • Identity & access
  • Networking
  • Backup & ransomware readiness
  • Business continuity planning
  • Field-site OT/IT hygiene
