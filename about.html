About

BlueScale Security is a West Texas practice for security, continuity, and identity — built for oil & gas, agriculture, and local SMBs.

Who we are

Raised in the Permian Basin and steeped in oilfield and West Texas life, our team brings over ten years of systems engineering experience — hands-on with compliance, disaster recovery, networking, identity and access (IAM), and business continuity plans (BCPs). We’re passionate about security, infrastructure, and optimizing workflows and procedures wherever they slow a shop down. The work stays practical: help shops tighten what matters without theater or fearmongering.

Who it's for

Oil & gas, agriculture, and local SMBs that want a practical defensive security partner — one place to go for assessment and hardening, identity, networking, backup and ransomware readiness, and business continuity planning. If you already have IT help in place, that’s fine — we can still work with you.

What we focus on

  • Security assessment & hardening
  • Identity & access
  • Backup & ransomware readiness
  • Business continuity planning
  • OT/IT networking & site hygiene
