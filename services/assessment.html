Service

Authorized security assessment

A scoped, permissioned review of how your systems and practices look today — so you know what to fix first.

Who it’s for

Operators and owners in oil & gas, agriculture, and local SMBs who want a clear picture without hiring a full-time security team. Useful before insurance questionnaires, MSP handoffs, or a hardening project.

What you get

  • Written findings prioritized by impact and effort
  • A short conversation about what’s realistic for your shop
  • High-level recommendations you can act on or assign
  • Scope documented up front — what’s in, what’s out

What it’s not

This is not unauthorized scanning, “gotcha” hacking, or exploit demonstration. We don’t run offensive pen-test theater. Assessment work is authorized, defensive, and aimed at hygiene and readiness.
