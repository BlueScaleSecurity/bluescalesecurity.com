Service

Networking

Practical network hygiene, segmentation, and remote-site connectivity — so oilfield and ag sites stay usable and harder to knock over.

Who it's for

Operators and SMBs with offices, yards, and remote sites that share internet, VPN, or vendor access. You want clear zones and sane remote access — not a science project.

What you get

  • Review of how sites, offices, and vendors connect today
  • Practical segmentation recommendations (office vs OT-ish gear vs guest / vendor paths)
  • Remote-site connectivity hygiene: VPN, firewall rules you can explain, and least-privilege access for partners
  • A plain punch list your IT or MSP can implement without overnight drama

What it's not

This is defensive network hygiene and design — not penetration testing, wireless cracking, traffic interception demos, or attack playbooks. We do not sell "how to break in."
