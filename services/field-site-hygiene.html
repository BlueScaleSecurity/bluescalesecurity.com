Service

Field-site OT/IT hygiene

Practical separation and hygiene for oilfield and ag sites — where corporate IT, vendor laptops, and OT-adjacent gear share space.

Who it is for

Oil & gas and agriculture operations with remote or field locations: shared Wi-Fi habits, vendor access, remote desktop paths, and gear that was not designed for a modern office network.

What you get

  • A plain map of how IT and OT-adjacent systems connect today
  • Hygiene recommendations: segmentation where it helps, account control, remote access defaults
  • Vendor and laptop practices that reduce accidental crossover
  • Priorities that respect uptime and field reality

What it is not

This is not ICS exploit research, PLC attack demos, or unauthorized probing of production controls. Work is defensive hygiene and architecture guidance only.
