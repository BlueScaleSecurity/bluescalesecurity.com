Service

Backups

Backup design you can trust — including proving restores work — with ransomware-aware hygiene so one bad day does not wipe the only copy.

Who it's for

Shops that "have backups" but have not restored lately, or that keep backups on the same network path an attacker would reach first.

What you get

  • Review of what is backed up, where it lives, and how often
  • Restore verification plan (test restores that fit your downtime tolerance)
  • Ransomware-aware hygiene: offline / immutable options, separation from day-to-day servers, and who can delete backups
  • Clear owners and a short runbook for "we need a file / a server / a site back"

What it's not

Backup work is recovery design and discipline — not malware analysis, decryptor hunting, or paying/negotiating with threat actors. We focus on getting your data back under your control.
