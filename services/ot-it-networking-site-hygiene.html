Service

OT/IT networking & site hygiene

Practical segmentation, remote-site connectivity, and field hygiene — so offices, yards, and OT-adjacent gear stay usable and harder to knock over.

Who it's for

Oil & gas and agriculture operations with offices, yards, and remote sites — shared internet, VPN, vendor laptops, and gear that was not designed for a modern office network. You want clear zones and sane remote access, not a science project.

What you get

  • A plain map of how offices, field sites, vendors, and OT-adjacent systems connect today
  • Practical segmentation: office vs OT-ish gear vs guest / vendor paths
  • Remote-site connectivity hygiene — VPN, firewall rules you can explain, least-privilege partner access
  • Vendor and laptop practices that reduce accidental crossover
  • A punch list that respects uptime and field reality

What it's not

Defensive network and site hygiene only — not ICS exploit research, PLC attack demos, wireless cracking, traffic interception, or unauthorized probing of production controls. We do not sell "how to break in."
