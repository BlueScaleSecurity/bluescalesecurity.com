Service

Business continuity planning

BCP/DR lite for SMBs — who does what when systems or sites go down, so ops keep moving without a binder nobody opens.

Who it's for

Oil & gas and ag businesses that cannot afford long outages but do not need a Fortune-500 continuity binder. You want a short, lived-in plan.

What you get

  • Map of critical ops (what must stay up, what can wait, who decides)
  • Lightweight incident and outage playbooks for common failures (power, ISP, ransomware lockout, key person out)
  • Communication tree: who calls customers, vendors, insurance, and IT
  • Tabletop walkthrough so the plan is practiced once, not just filed

What it's not

This is practical continuity planning — not fearmongering scenarios, red-team war games, or a binder of unused policies. We keep it short enough that people will actually use it.
