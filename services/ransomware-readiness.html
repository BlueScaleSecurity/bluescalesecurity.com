Service

Ransomware readiness

Make sure backups, restores, and first-response steps are real — before you need them on a Sunday night.

Who it’s for

Companies that can’t afford long downtime: producers, ag operations, and SMBs where payroll, SCADA-adjacent systems, or customer data sit on a thin stack.

What you get

  • Review of backup coverage, retention, and offline/immutable options where they fit
  • Restore confidence checks — can you actually bring key systems back?
  • A short, plain response outline: who to call, what to protect first
  • Gaps called out without scare tactics

What it’s not

We don’t demonstrate ransomware, share payloads, or walk through attacker playbooks. Readiness here means defense, recovery, and communication — not offense.
