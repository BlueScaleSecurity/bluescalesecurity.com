Service

Backup & ransomware readiness

Backup design you can trust, proven restores, and first-response basics — so one bad day does not wipe the only copy or leave you guessing on a Sunday night.

Who it's for

Producers, ag operations, and SMBs that “have backups” but have not restored lately — or that can’t afford long downtime when payroll, field systems, or customer data sit on a thin stack.

What you get

  • Review of what is backed up, where it lives, how often, and retention that matches real needs
  • Restore verification — test restores that fit your downtime tolerance so recovery is proven, not assumed
  • Ransomware-aware backup hygiene: offline / immutable options where they fit, separation from day-to-day servers, and who can delete backups
  • Clear owners and a short runbook for “we need a file / a server / a site back”
  • A plain response outline: who to call, what to protect first, gaps called out without scare tactics

What it's not

We don’t demonstrate ransomware, share payloads, hunt decryptors for you, or walk through attacker playbooks. This is defense, recovery design, and communication — not offense or ransom negotiation.
