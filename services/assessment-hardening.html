Service

Security assessment & hardening

Find what’s weak, then fix what matters first — a scoped, authorized review that turns into a prioritized hardening punch list your shop can actually run.

Who it's for

Oil & gas, agriculture, and local SMBs that want a clear picture of how they’re set up today — and sensible baselines for accounts, devices, servers, and cloud — without a full-time security team or pen-test theater.

What you get

  • Authorized assessment of your environment with findings prioritized by impact and effort
  • Hardening recommendations for identity, endpoints, servers, and cloud settings that fit how you work
  • A ranked punch list you can implement in-house or with your MSP
  • Scope documented up front — what’s in, what’s out — and notes on what to leave alone so production stays up
  • A short conversation about what’s realistic for your size and schedule

What it's not

This is not unauthorized scanning, “gotcha” hacking, exploit demos, or red-team theater. Assessment and hardening here are authorized, defensive, and aimed at hygiene you can maintain.
