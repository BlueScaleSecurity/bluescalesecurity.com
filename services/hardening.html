Service

Hardening & configuration review

Tighten the settings that matter — accounts, devices, servers, and cloud — to a baseline that fits how you work.

Who it\u2019s for

Shops that already know they need better defaults: multi-factor where it counts, least privilege that ops can live with, and configs that don\u2019t drift into \u201cwe\u2019ll fix it later.\u201d

What you get

  • Review of identity, endpoint, server, and cloud configuration against practical baselines
  • A punch list of changes ranked by risk and effort
  • Guidance you can implement in-house or with your MSP
  • Notes on what to leave alone so production stays up

What it\u2019s not

Hardening is configuration and process work — not red-team exercises, malware samples, or exploit walkthroughs. We focus on defensive defaults and maintainable settings.
